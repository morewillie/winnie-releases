# Winnie

A plush goat who lives on your Mac desktop. She chats, remembers what you tell her, and runs entirely on your computer: no account, no API key.

Download the DMG from [Releases](https://github.com/morewillie/winnie-releases/releases/latest), open it, and drag Winnie to Applications.

Requires macOS 13 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel). On first launch Winnie downloads her local language model (about 1.9 GB) once.
